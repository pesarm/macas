Since 1884

A Street with a Story

1884

The First Village

Praia das Maçãs was founded as the first seaside village on the Sintra coast. A Spanish priest, Padre António Mathias del Campo, built the very first structure — and a village was born.

1889

Portugal's National Anthem

Alfredo Keil — composer of A Portuguesa, Portugal's national anthem — built his summer villa here in 1889. He even had a private chapel built next door for the village's first mass.

~1900

The Prego Sandwich

Manuel Dias Prego worked as a beach attendant right here and started serving fried veal steaks in a bread roll. Portugal's most iconic sandwich — the prego no pão — takes its name from him.

20th c.

Eugène Lévi Avenue

The villa stands on Avenida Eugène Lévi — named after the Jewish entrepreneur who shaped Praia das Maçãs into the seaside destination it is today. The street carries his name. So does the address of this house.

Where You'll Be

Praia das Maçãs is a charming seaside village on the Sintra coast, 40km west of Lisbon. The beach is perfect for families — calm in summer, with surf waves in autumn and spring.

Take the vintage tram to Sintra (15 min) for world-class palaces and UNESCO gardens. Drive 10 minutes to Cabo da Roca — Europe's westernmost point — or walk to the natural pools at Azenhas do Mar.

💡 Tip: Uber and taxis are easy and affordable. Lisbon airport is 45 minutes away.
