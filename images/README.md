## How to get your Airbnb photos

1. Open your Airbnb listing
2. Click each photo → right-click → Save Image As
3. Rename and save here

## Tips

- JPG or WebP format, ideally 1200–2000px wide
- Landscape orientation works best for the gallery grid
- hero.jpg should be at least 1920×1080px
